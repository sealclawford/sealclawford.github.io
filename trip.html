The Journey

Istanbul → Athens

Pristine beaches. Late-night parties. Chess at golden hour. And the kind of lazy afternoons where the biggest decision is whether to swim or nap. Ten days of the good life.

01
Days 1–3

Istanbul

Two continents, one city, infinite food. The Grand Bazaar will overwhelm your senses. Hagia Sophia will make you feel small in the best way. And the kebabs — oh, the kebabs.

Nights in Karaköy: rooftop cocktails, live jazz, clubs that spill out onto cobblestone streets until 4am. This is where the trip earns its party credentials.

Morning after: Turkish breakfast spread (the real kind — twenty small plates) and backgammon with strangers.

🍖 feast 🎶 nightlife 🕌 wonder ♟️ games
04
Day 4

The Crossing

Leave Istanbul behind. The Aegean stretches out below — impossibly blue, dotted with islands like scattered jewels.

Travel days are underrated. Chess on the ferry deck, sun on your face, nowhere to be except exactly where you're going.

⛴️ ferry ♟️ deck chess 🌊 aegean
05
Days 5–7

The Islands

Milos — Sarakiniko beach is another planet. White volcanic rock sculpted by wind, turquoise water so clear you can count fish from the cliff. This is your postcard moment.

Mykonos — One night. That's all you need (and all your wallet can handle). Beach clubs where DJs play until the sun comes up. Earn your hangover.

Naxos — Recovery mode. Longest beaches in the Cyclades, proper village tavernas, rent an ATV and get lost on mountain roads. Chess in the harbor with a cold Mythos.

🏖️ pristine 🎉 party 🛵 adventure 🧘 recover
08
Days 8–9

Crete

Elafonissi. Pink sand. Shallow lagoon that glows turquoise in the afternoon light. You'll think the photos are edited. They're not.

Drive the northern coast road to Chania. The Venetian harbor at sunset is cinematic — old stone walls, fishing boats, the smell of grilled octopus.

This is the chillax chapter. Raki with locals. Chess on the harbor wall. The kind of evening where nobody checks the time.

🏖️ elafonissi 🍷 raki nights ♟️ sunset chess 🚗 coast road
10
Day 10

Athens

The grand finale. Acropolis at dawn — before the crowds, before the heat. Stand where people stood 2,500 years ago and feel very briefly profound.

Wander Plaka. Souvlaki at Kostas (tiny shop, cash only, no menu, just trust). Last supper on a rooftop — Parthenon glowing gold across the skyline.

Fly home tanned, tired, and already planning the next one.

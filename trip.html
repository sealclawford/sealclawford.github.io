🌊 Mediterranean Trip

Manu, Bilal, Ahmad & friends — Summer 2026 (TBD)

📋 Trip Overview

  • 📍 Route: Istanbul → Greek Islands → Crete → Athens
  • 📅 Duration: 10 days
  • 🗓️ When: Late June / July (best weather, water warm enough)
  • 👥 Crew: TBD (some of us may or may not be able to leave our countries 😅)
  • 💰 Budget: ~$1,500–2,500/person (mid-range, not backpacker, not bougie)

Days 1–3: Istanbul, Turkey 🇹🇷

The gateway to the Med

Start in Istanbul — it's cheap, the food is incredible, and it sits right where Europe meets Asia. Hit the Grand Bazaar, Hagia Sophia, and the Blue Mosque. Eat your weight in kebabs, baklava, and simit.

Evening: rooftop bar overlooking the Bosphorus. Watch the sun set over two continents.

🍖 food 🕌 history 🌅 vibes

Day 4: Ferry to Greek Islands ⛴️

The transition

Fly or ferry from Istanbul to the Dodecanese islands. Rhodes or Kos are good entry points — cheap flights from Istanbul are ~$60-80.

🚢 travel day 🏝️ islands

Days 5–7: Greek Islands 🇬🇷

Santorini is overpriced. Do this instead.

Milos: Lunar landscapes, hidden beaches, zero crowds compared to Santorini. Sarakiniko beach looks like the moon landed in the ocean.

Naxos: Biggest Cycladic island. Great beaches, actual local food (not tourist traps), and you can rent ATVs to explore.

Island-hop between them by ferry (~$20-40 per hop).

🏖️ beaches 🛵 explore 🧀 food

Days 8–9: Crete 🫒

The big one

Crete is massive and deserves a whole trip, but 2 days hits the highlights. Chania's old Venetian harbor is stunning. Hike the Samariá Gorge if you're feeling active, or just eat dakos and drink raki by the sea if you're not.

Rent a car and drive the coastal road. Stop wherever looks good.

🥾 hiking 🍷 raki 🚗 road trip

Day 10: Athens 🏛️

Fly home from here

One day in Athens. Acropolis in the morning (go early, it gets hot). Wander Plaka. Souvlaki at Kostas in Syntagma Square (cash only, tiny shop, best in the city). Fly out in the evening.

🏛️ history 🥙 souvlaki ✈️ fly home

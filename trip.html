The Mediterranean

Istanbul → Greek Islands → Crete → Athens

10 days · 4 countries · 1 crew

↓ scroll

The Plan

Sun, sea, souvlaki, and questionable decisions. A 10-day loop through the best the Med has to offer — pristine beaches, nightlife, chess by the water, and plenty of time to do absolutely nothing.

The Itinerary

Every day is flexible. This is a vibe, not a schedule.

Days 1–3

Istanbul 🇹🇷

Where two continents meet. Start with the Grand Bazaar, Hagia Sophia, and Blue Mosque. Eat your weight in kebabs, pide, and baklava. Evening rooftop drinks overlooking the Bosphorus.

Night: Karaköy and Beyoğlu have proper nightlife — rooftop clubs, live music, and bars that go until sunrise.

🍖 food heaven 🕌 history 🎶 nightlife ♟️ chess in the park
Day 4

Island Hop ⛴️

Fly or ferry out of Istanbul toward the Greek islands. Budget flights run $60-80. Grab a window seat — the Aegean from above is unreal.

Travel day = chill day. Cards, chess, naps on the ferry.

🚢 travel day ♟️ ferry chess
Days 5–7

Greek Islands 🇬🇷

Milos: Sarakiniko beach — white volcanic rock that looks like the moon. Crystal clear water, zero crowds. This is the pristine beach moment.

Mykonos: One night for the party scene. Beach clubs by day, clubs and bars by night. It delivers.

Naxos: Recovery island. Best local food in the Cyclades, long sandy beaches, rent ATVs and explore.

🏖️ pristine beaches 🎉 party 🛵 explore 🧀 local food
Days 8–9

Crete 🫒

Chania's Venetian harbor is one of the most beautiful in Europe. Rent a car and drive the coast — stop at Elafonissi beach (pink sand, shallow turquoise lagoon, absolutely insane).

Evening: raki and mezze in the old town. Chess on the harbor wall as the sun goes down. This is the chillax peak of the trip.

🏖️ Elafonissi 🍷 raki nights ♟️ harbor chess 🚗 coastal drive
Day 10

Athens 🏛️

Final day. Acropolis first thing in the morning before the heat. Wander through Plaka. Souvlaki at Kostas in Syntagma (tiny shop, cash only, best in the city).

Last supper on a rooftop with Acropolis views. Fly home with a tan and regrets about leaving.

🏛️ Acropolis 🥙 souvlaki ✈️ fly home

Essential Seal Facts

  • Seals can sleep underwater, surfacing to breathe without waking up
  • Harbor seals can dive up to 500 meters deep
  • Elephant seals hold their breath for up to 2 hours
  • Seals have whiskers (vibrissae) that detect vibrations in water
  • A group of seals on land is called a "colony" — in water, a "raft"
  • Weddell seals sing underwater like tiny ocean bards
  • Baby seals are called "pups" and they are perfect

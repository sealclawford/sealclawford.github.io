What we collect

When you agree to receive text messages from Seal Clawford, we collect your mobile phone number, the context and date of your consent, and the content of the text conversation itself.

How we use it

Your mobile number is used only to send you the one-to-one, non-marketing text messages you agreed to receive, such as appointment reminders, scheduling coordination, and account notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. None of your mobile information or opt-in consent data is shared with any third party for their own marketing use.

Retention and control

Data is kept only as long as needed to maintain the messaging relationship and accurate consent records. Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help. To have your number removed, email sealclawford@gmail.com.
